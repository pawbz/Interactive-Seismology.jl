# Grading
Assignments (40%); Mid term presentations (30%); End term exam (30%)

## Notebook Review Report Assignments

Choose and explore at least **10 interactive notebooks** from the course website. Submit a **handwritten report** with one clearly labelled entry for each chosen notebook.

For each notebook, your entry should include:

- The notebook title and the widget you explored.
- A concise explanation of the scientific demonstration: what physical concept the widget illustrates, which controls you changed, and what you observed.
- **Two concrete suggestions** to improve the widget. Each suggestion should explain why it would improve the scientific demonstration, clarity, usability, or interpretation.
- One possible physics bug, limitation, or questionable assumption. Explain the reasoning behind your concern; you do not need to prove that it is an error.

Marks will be awarded primarily for the quality of the two suggested improvements: their logic, relevance to the notebook, and connection to the science being demonstrated. Additional bonus marks will be awarded for identifying a plausible physics bug or limitation and supporting it with sound reasoning.

## Mid/End Term Exams
Open book exams, where you will have access to textbook chapters/ other reading material. The exam duration will be 3 Hrs.
The only thing that will not be allowed is interaction with peers.
