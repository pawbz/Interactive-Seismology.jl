# Recommended textbooks

These texts complement the notebooks at different levels. Start with Shearer or
Stein and Wysession; use Aki and Richards or Dahlen and Tromp when you want a
more mathematical treatment.

- **Shearer, P. M. (2019). _Introduction to Seismology_ (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.** A clear first textbook on seismic waves, earthquakes, seismic sources, and imaging.
- **Stein, S., & Wysession, M. (2003). _An Introduction to Seismology, Earthquakes, and Earth Structure_. Blackwell Publishing.** A broad, physical introduction connecting seismology to plate tectonics and Earth structure.
- **Aki, K., & Richards, P. G. (2002). _Quantitative Seismology_ (2nd ed.). University Science Books.** The standard advanced reference for wave propagation, source theory, and seismic observations.
- **Dahlen, F. A., & Tromp, J. (1998). _Theoretical Global Seismology_. Princeton University Press.** A rigorous reference for normal modes, ray theory, and global seismic wavefields.
- **Lowrie, W., & Fichtner, A. (2020). _Fundamentals of Geophysics_ (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.** Useful for placing seismology alongside gravity, magnetics, geodesy, and other geophysical methods.
